The 2022 CDC Clinical Practice Guideline for Prescribing Opioids for Pain (2022 Clinical Practice Guideline) guides clinicians to work together with patients to make informed, patient-centered decisions about pain care.

The 2022 Clinical Practice Guideline includes 12 recommendations for clinicians providing pain care for outpatients aged 18 years or older with acute pain, subacute pain, or chronic pain.

This guideline provides recommendations only. It does not replace clinical judgment and individualized, patient-centered decision-making.
