Mpox vaccination — Special situations

Age 18 years and at risk for mpox infection: complete 2-dose series, 28 days apart.

Risk factors for mpox infection include:

Persons who are gay or bisexual or other MSM, transgender, or nonbinary people who in the past 6 months have had:

A new diagnosis of at least 1 sexually transmitted disease
More than 1 sex partner
Sex at a commercial sex venue
Sex in association with a large public event in a geographic area where mpox transmission is occurring

Persons who are sexual partners of the persons described above
Persons who anticipate experiencing any of the situations described above

Pregnancy: There is currently no ACIP recommendation for Jynneos use in pregnancy due to lack of safety data in pregnant women. Pregnant women with any risk factor described above may receive Jynneos

For detailed information, see: https://www.cdc.gov/monkeypox/hcp/vaccine-considerations/
