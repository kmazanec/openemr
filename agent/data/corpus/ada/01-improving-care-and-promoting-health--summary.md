Improving individual and population health for people with and at risk for diabetes requires engagement of and collaboration between people with diabetes and their caregivers, interprofessional health care teams, health systems, community partners, payors, policymakers, and public health agencies. This section provides guidance to facilitate implementation of evidence-based diabetes care recommendations that are discussed in the Standards of Care with the goal of improving health, eliminating health disparities, and reducing the impact of diabetes and its complications on individuals and society.
