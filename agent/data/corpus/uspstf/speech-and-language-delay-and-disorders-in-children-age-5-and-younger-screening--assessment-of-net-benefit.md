The USPSTF concludes that the evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for speech and language delay and disorders in children who do not present with signs or symptoms or parent/caregiver concerns. This is not a recommendation for or against screening, and the USPSTF is calling for more research on the benefits and harms of screening.
