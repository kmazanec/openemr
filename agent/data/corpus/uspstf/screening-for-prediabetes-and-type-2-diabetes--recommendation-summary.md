Population | Recommendation | Grade
Asymptomatic adults aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity | The USPSTF recommends screening for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes in adults aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity. Clinicians should offer or refer patients with prediabetes to effective preventive interventions. | B
