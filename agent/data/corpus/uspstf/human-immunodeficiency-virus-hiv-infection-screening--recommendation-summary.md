Population | Recommendation | Grade
Pregnant persons | The USPSTF recommends that clinicians screen for HIV infection in all pregnant persons, including those who present in labor or at delivery whose HIV status is unknown. | A
Adolescents and adults aged 15 to 65 years | The USPSTF recommends that clinicians screen for HIV infection in adolescents and adults aged 15 to 65 years. Younger adolescents and older adults who are at increased risk of infection should also be screened.
See the Clinical Considerations section for more information about assessment of risk, screening intervals, and rescreening in pregnancy. | A
