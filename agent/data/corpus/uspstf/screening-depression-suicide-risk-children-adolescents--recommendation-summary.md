Population | Recommendation | Grade
Adolescents aged 12 to 18 years | The USPSTF recommends screening for major depressive disorder (MDD) in adolescents aged 12 to 18 years. | B
Children 11 years or younger | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for MDD in children 11 years or younger.

See the Practice Considerations section for additional information regarding the I statement. | I
Children and adolescents | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for suicide risk in children and adolescents.

See the Practice Considerations section for additional information regarding the I statement. | I
