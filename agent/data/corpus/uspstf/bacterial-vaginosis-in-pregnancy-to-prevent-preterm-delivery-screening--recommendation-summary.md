Population | Recommendation | Grade
Pregnant persons not at increased risk for preterm delivery | The USPSTF recommends against screening for bacterial vaginosis (BV) in pregnant persons who are not at increased risk for preterm delivery. | D
Pregnant persons at increased risk for preterm delivery | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for BV in pregnant persons who are at increased risk for preterm delivery. | I
