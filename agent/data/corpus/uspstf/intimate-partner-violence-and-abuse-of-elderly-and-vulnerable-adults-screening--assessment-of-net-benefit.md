The USPSTF concludes with moderate certainty that screening for IPV in women of reproductive age, including those who are pregnant and postpartum, and providing or referring those who screen positive to multicomponent interventions with ongoing support has a moderate net benefit.
The USPSTF concludes that the evidence is insufficient on screening for caregiver abuse and neglect in older and vulnerable adults and that the balance of benefits and harms cannot be determined.
See Table 1 for more information on the USPSTF recommendation rationale and assessment. For more details on the methods the USPSTF uses to determine the net benefit, see the USPSTF Procedure Manual.8
