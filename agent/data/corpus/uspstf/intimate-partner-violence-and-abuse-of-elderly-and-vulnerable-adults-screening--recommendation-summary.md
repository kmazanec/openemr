Population | Recommendation | Grade
Women of reproductive age, including pregnant and postpartum women | The USPSTF recommends that clinicians screen for intimate partner violence (IPV) in women of reproductive age, including those who are pregnant and postpartum.

See the "Practice Considerations" section for information on evidence-based multicomponent interventions and for information on IPV in men. | B
Older or vulnerable adults | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening for caregiver abuse and neglect in older or vulnerable adults.

See the "Practice Considerations" section for additional information. | I
