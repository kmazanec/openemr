Population | Recommendation | Grade
Children aged 3 to 5 years | The USPSTF recommends vision screening at least once in all children aged 3 to 5 years to detect amblyopia or its risk factors. | B
Children younger than 3 years | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of vision screening in children younger than 3 years. | I
