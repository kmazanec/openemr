Population | Recommendation | Grade
Women aged 40 to 74 years | The USPSTF recommends biennial screening mammography for women aged 40 to 74 years. | B
Women 75 years or older | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of screening mammography in women 75 years or older. | I
Women with dense breasts | The USPSTF concludes that the current evidence is insufficient to assess the balance of benefits and harms of supplemental screening for breast cancer using breast ultrasonography or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) in women identified to have dense breasts on an otherwise negative screening mammogram.

See the "Practice Considerations" section for more information on the patient population to whom this recommendation applies and on screening mammography modalities. | I
