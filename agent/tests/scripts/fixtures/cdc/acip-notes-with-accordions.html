Child Immunization Schedule Notes

July 2, 2025

Influenza vaccination

Age 6 months or older: 1 dose any influenza vaccine annually.

Egg allergy: any age-appropriate influenza vaccine may be used.

MMR vaccination

Age 12 months: 1st dose. Age 4 through 6 years: 2nd dose.

Minimum interval between doses: 4 weeks.
