Hypertension Management Program (HMP) Toolkit

August 12, 2024

At a glance

The Hypertension Management Program (HMP) is an evidence-based protocol for clinical teams to treat patients with hypertension. The toolkit equips primary-care practices to implement standardized BP measurement and titration.

Purpose

HMP improves hypertension control rates by aligning provider workflow with evidence-based BP-control protocols. The toolkit covers measurement, medication titration, lifestyle counseling, and follow-up cadence.

Overview

HMP is structured into four modules that map to the standard clinical workflow: identify, treat, monitor, and follow up. Each module ships with worksheets, EHR templates, and patient-facing handouts.
